Research Group

Mentored, small-group research that takes you from your very first question all the way to a published paper.

What it is

Real research, with people beside you

A Research Group is a small team of students who work on a research project together, guided by a mentor. It's free, friendly, and built so that anyone — whatever your starting point — can do real research and share it with the world.

Small groups

You'll work in a small team, so there's always someone to bounce ideas off and no one gets lost in the crowd.

A real mentor

An experienced mentor guides your group through each step, answering questions in plain language as they come up.

A published paper

The goal is a finished, peer-reviewed paper you can be proud of — published free for the whole world to read.

How it works

From question to published

Four simple steps, with mentors and peers helping you the whole way.

1

Join the community

Hop into our Discord and say hello. It takes a minute and costs nothing.

2

Get matched

We'll match you to a research group and a mentor based on what you're curious about.

3

Do the research

Shape a question, gather your data, and work through it together, step by step.

4

Publish your work

Submit to our peer-reviewed journal and share your discovery with the world.

Who it's for

Made for curious students

You don't need a fancy background or any experience. If you're curious and willing to learn, there's a place for you here — alongside 1000+ researchers in 93+ countries.

First-time researchers

Never written a paper before? Perfect. Your mentor and group will walk you through it from scratch.

Idea-havers

Got a question that won't leave you alone? Bring it. A Research Group is a great place to chase it down.

Students everywhere

Wherever you are in the world, you can join. Everything happens online and stays completely free.

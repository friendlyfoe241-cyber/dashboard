Partnerships

Let's grow research together

Synthica is a free, global community of 1000+ young researchers across 93+ countries. We love teaming up with people and organizations who want to open research up to more students — in plain language, at zero cost to members.

Schools & Chapters

Start a Synthica chapter at your school and give your students mentors, peers, and a real path to publishing — all completely free.

Nonprofits & NGOs

Reach more young people with hands-on research opportunities. We'll plug your community into our global network of 1000+ researchers.

Universities & Labs

Mentor the next generation, share resources, or open your doors to high school researchers from 93+ countries who are eager to learn.

The Synthica Journal

A free, peer-reviewed journal built for high school researchers — with no submission fees, ever.

About the Journal

Publishing made possible

The Synthica Journal gives high school students a credible, supportive place to publish original research. Every submission gets genuine peer review and editorial guidance — not a pay-to-publish shortcut. Authors from our community of 1000+ researchers in 93+ countries are welcome.

For Authors

Before you submit

  • Your manuscript reports original, student-led research.
  • You've followed the formatting and citation guidelines.
  • All authors and mentors are acknowledged.
  • Data and methods are described clearly enough to reproduce.
  • You've read and agree to our ethics and integrity policies.

Aims & Scope

We publish original research across the sciences, mathematics, social sciences, and humanities, authored or co-authored by high school students.

Our goal is to make scholarly publishing approachable: clear expectations, plain-language feedback, and editors who want first-time authors to succeed.

Policies

We hold every submission to high standards of research integrity, honest authorship, and ethical conduct. Plagiarism and fabricated data are never tolerated.

Peer review is constructive and educational. There are no submission, review, or publication fees — access is free for both authors and readers.
